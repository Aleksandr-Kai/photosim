FAQ

What is the depth of field of the Canon EF 400mm F2.8L IS III USM at f/2.8 and 3 m?

At 400mm, f/2.8 and focus at 3 m, acceptable sharpness runs from 3.00 to 3.00 m — about 1 cm in total.

What is the hyperfocal distance of the Canon EF 400mm F2.8L IS III USM?

At 400mm and f/8 on a Full Frame sensor the hyperfocal distance is about 694 m. Focus there and everything from 347 m to infinity is sharp.

How do I get more background blur with the Canon EF 400mm F2.8L IS III USM?

Open the aperture to f/2.8, move closer to your subject and increase the distance between subject and background. You can check the result in the calculator above.
