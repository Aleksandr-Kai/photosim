Sigma 18-35mm F1.8 DC HSM Art — depth of field calculator

The Sigma 18-35mm F1.8 DC HSM Art is a zoom lens for the Third-Party (Various Mounts) system with a focal length of 18–35mm, a maximum aperture of f/1.8 and a minimum aperture of f/16. Below is a ready-to-use depth of field (DoF) calculator and tables for typical shooting distances, computed with the same formulas as the PhotoSim app.

Key numbers

  • Mount / system: Third-Party (Various Mounts)
  • Sensor: APS-C, 23.5 × 15.6mm
  • Focal length: 18–35mm (about 37mm full-frame equivalent, 1.53× crop)
  • Aperture: f/1.8 to f/16
  • DoF at 3 m, 24mm, f/1.8: 2.55–3.63 m (≈ 108 cm deep)

FAQ

What is the depth of field of the Sigma 18-35mm F1.8 DC HSM Art at f/1.8 and 3 m?

At 24mm, f/1.8 and focus at 3 m, acceptable sharpness runs from 2.55 to 3.63 m — about 108 cm in total.

What is the hyperfocal distance of the Sigma 18-35mm F1.8 DC HSM Art?

At 24mm and f/8 on a APS-C sensor the hyperfocal distance is about 3.85 m. Focus there and everything from 1.93 m to infinity is sharp.

How do I get more background blur with the Sigma 18-35mm F1.8 DC HSM Art?

Open the aperture to f/1.8, move closer to your subject and increase the distance between subject and background, and zoom in to the long end. You can check the result in the calculator above.
