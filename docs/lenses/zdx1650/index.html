FAQ

What is the depth of field of the Nikon Z DX 16-50mm f/3.5-6.3 VR at f/4.5 and 3 m?

At 28mm, f/4.5 and focus at 3 m, acceptable sharpness runs from 2.27 to 4.41 m — about 214 cm in total.

What is the hyperfocal distance of the Nikon Z DX 16-50mm f/3.5-6.3 VR?

At 28mm and f/8 on a APS-C sensor the hyperfocal distance is about 5.24 m. Focus there and everything from 2.62 m to infinity is sharp.

How do I get more background blur with the Nikon Z DX 16-50mm f/3.5-6.3 VR?

Open the aperture to f/4.5, move closer to your subject and increase the distance between subject and background, and zoom in to the long end. You can check the result in the calculator above.
