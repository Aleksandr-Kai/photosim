Fujifilm XF 35mm F1.4 R — depth of field calculator

The Fujifilm XF 35mm F1.4 R is a prime lens for the Fujifilm X (APS-C) system with a focal length of 35mm, a maximum aperture of f/1.4 and a minimum aperture of f/16. Below is a ready-to-use depth of field (DoF) calculator and tables for typical shooting distances, computed with the same formulas as the PhotoSim app.

Key numbers

  • Mount / system: Fujifilm X (APS-C)
  • Sensor: APS-C, 23.5 × 15.6mm
  • Focal length: 35mm (about 54mm full-frame equivalent, 1.53× crop)

FAQ

What is the depth of field of the Fujifilm XF 35mm F1.4 R at f/1.4 and 3 m?

At 35mm, f/1.4 and focus at 3 m, acceptable sharpness runs from 2.82 to 3.20 m — about 38 cm in total.

What is the hyperfocal distance of the Fujifilm XF 35mm F1.4 R?

At 35mm and f/8 on a APS-C sensor the hyperfocal distance is about 8.18 m. Focus there and everything from 4.09 m to infinity is sharp.

How do I get more background blur with the Fujifilm XF 35mm F1.4 R?

Open the aperture to f/1.4, move closer to your subject and increase the distance between subject and background. You can check the result in the calculator above.
