FAQ

What is the depth of field of the Canon EF 100-400mm F4.5-5.6L IS II USM at f/4.9 and 3 m?

At 200mm, f/4.9 and focus at 3 m, acceptable sharpness runs from 2.97 to 3.03 m — about 6 cm in total.

What is the hyperfocal distance of the Canon EF 100-400mm F4.5-5.6L IS II USM?

At 200mm and f/8 on a Full Frame sensor the hyperfocal distance is about 174 m. Focus there and everything from 86.8 m to infinity is sharp.

How do I get more background blur with the Canon EF 100-400mm F4.5-5.6L IS II USM?

Open the aperture to f/4.9, move closer to your subject and increase the distance between subject and background, and zoom in to the long end. You can check the result in the calculator above.
