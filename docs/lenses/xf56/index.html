Fujifilm XF 56mm F1.2 R — depth of field calculator

The Fujifilm XF 56mm F1.2 R is a prime lens for the Fujifilm X (APS-C) system with a focal length of 56mm, a maximum aperture of f/1.2 and a minimum aperture of f/16. Below is a ready-to-use depth of field (DoF) calculator and tables for typical shooting distances, computed with the same formulas as the PhotoSim app.

Key numbers

  • Mount / system: Fujifilm X (APS-C)
  • Sensor: APS-C, 23.5 × 15.6mm
  • Focal length: 56mm (about 86mm full-frame equivalent, 1.53× crop)

FAQ

What is the depth of field of the Fujifilm XF 56mm F1.2 R at f/1.2 and 3 m?

At 56mm, f/1.2 and focus at 3 m, acceptable sharpness runs from 2.94 to 3.06 m — about 13 cm in total.

What is the hyperfocal distance of the Fujifilm XF 56mm F1.2 R?

At 56mm and f/8 on a APS-C sensor the hyperfocal distance is about 20.9 m. Focus there and everything from 10.5 m to infinity is sharp.

How do I get more background blur with the Fujifilm XF 56mm F1.2 R?

Open the aperture to f/1.2, move closer to your subject and increase the distance between subject and background. You can check the result in the calculator above.
