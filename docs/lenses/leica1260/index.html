FAQ

What is the depth of field of the Leica DG 12-60mm F2.8-4 at f/3.2 and 3 m?

At 28mm, f/3.2 and focus at 3 m, acceptable sharpness runs from 2.55 to 3.64 m — about 108 cm in total.

What is the hyperfocal distance of the Leica DG 12-60mm F2.8-4?

At 28mm and f/8 on a Micro Four Thirds sensor the hyperfocal distance is about 6.82 m. Focus there and everything from 3.41 m to infinity is sharp.

How do I get more background blur with the Leica DG 12-60mm F2.8-4?

Open the aperture to f/3.2, move closer to your subject and increase the distance between subject and background, and zoom in to the long end. You can check the result in the calculator above.
