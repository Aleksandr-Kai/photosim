The Fujifilm XF 8-16mm F2.8 R LM WR is a zoom lens for the Fujifilm X (APS-C) system with a focal length of 8–16mm, a maximum aperture of f/2.8 and a minimum aperture of f/22. Key numbers

  • Mount / system: Fujifilm X (APS-C)
  • Sensor: APS-C, 23.5 × 15.6mm

FAQ

What is the depth of field of the Fujifilm XF 8-16mm F2.8 R LM WR at f/2.8 and 3 m?

At 14mm, f/2.8 and focus at 3 m, acceptable sharpness runs from 1.67 to 14.9 m — about 1327 cm in total.

What is the hyperfocal distance of the Fujifilm XF 8-16mm F2.8 R LM WR?

At 14mm and f/8 on a APS-C sensor the hyperfocal distance is about 1.32 m. Focus there and everything from 0.66 m to infinity is sharp.

How do I get more background blur with the Fujifilm XF 8-16mm F2.8 R LM WR?

Open the aperture to f/2.8, move closer to your subject and increase the distance between subject and background, and zoom in to the long end. You can check the result in the calculator above.
