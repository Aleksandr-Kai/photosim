FAQ

What is the depth of field of the Canon RF-S 55-210mm F5-7.1 IS STM at f/5.6 and 3 m?

At 100mm, f/5.6 and focus at 3 m, acceptable sharpness runs from 2.91 to 3.09 m — about 18 cm in total.

What is the hyperfocal distance of the Canon RF-S 55-210mm F5-7.1 IS STM?

At 100mm and f/8 on a APS-C sensor the hyperfocal distance is about 66.6 m. Focus there and everything from 33.3 m to infinity is sharp.

How do I get more background blur with the Canon RF-S 55-210mm F5-7.1 IS STM?

Open the aperture to f/5.6, move closer to your subject and increase the distance between subject and background, and zoom in to the long end. You can check the result in the calculator above.
