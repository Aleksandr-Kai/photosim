FAQ

What is the depth of field of the Olympus M.Zuiko 25mm F1.8 at f/1.8 and 3 m?

At 25mm, f/1.8 and focus at 3 m, acceptable sharpness runs from 2.67 to 3.42 m — about 75 cm in total.

What is the hyperfocal distance of the Olympus M.Zuiko 25mm F1.8?

At 25mm and f/8 on a Micro Four Thirds sensor the hyperfocal distance is about 5.44 m. Focus there and everything from 2.72 m to infinity is sharp.

How do I get more background blur with the Olympus M.Zuiko 25mm F1.8?

Open the aperture to f/1.8, move closer to your subject and increase the distance between subject and background. You can check the result in the calculator above.
