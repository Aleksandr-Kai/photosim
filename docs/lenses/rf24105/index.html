FAQ

What is the depth of field of the Canon RF 24-105mm F4L IS USM at f/4 and 3 m?

At 50mm, f/4 and focus at 3 m, acceptable sharpness runs from 2.64 to 3.47 m — about 83 cm in total.

What is the hyperfocal distance of the Canon RF 24-105mm F4L IS USM?

At 50mm and f/8 on a Full Frame sensor the hyperfocal distance is about 10.9 m. Focus there and everything from 5.44 m to infinity is sharp.

How do I get more background blur with the Canon RF 24-105mm F4L IS USM?

Open the aperture to f/4, move closer to your subject and increase the distance between subject and background, and zoom in to the long end. You can check the result in the calculator above.
