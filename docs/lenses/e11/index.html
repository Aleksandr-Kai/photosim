FAQ

What is the depth of field of the Sony E 11mm F1.8 at f/1.8 and 3 m?

At 11mm, f/1.8 and focus at 3 m, acceptable sharpness runs from 1.64 to 18.0 m — about 1639 cm in total.

What is the hyperfocal distance of the Sony E 11mm F1.8?

At 11mm and f/8 on a APS-C sensor the hyperfocal distance is about 0.82 m. Focus there and everything from 0.41 m to infinity is sharp.

How do I get more background blur with the Sony E 11mm F1.8?

Open the aperture to f/1.8, move closer to your subject and increase the distance between subject and background. You can check the result in the calculator above.
