PhotoSim

See what stays sharp before you press the shutter. A depth-of-field simulator for Android, built from a real lens & sensor database.

Download for Android
v1.0.0 · APK · Android 6.0+

What it does

Pick a real camera lens and sensor from the built-in database, set how far you are from your subject, and PhotoSim draws the exact zone that will be sharp — near limit, far limit, and everything in between. Drag the subject or the camera right in the scene to see the sharp zone update live.

  • Real gear databaseOver a hundred lenses across Canon RF/EF, Nikon Z, Sony E, Fujifilm X, Micro Four Thirds, and third-party glass.
  • Live, to-scale sceneCamera and subject drawn at true relative size, on a fixed 10 m ruler — no distorted scale tricks.
  • Drag to focusMove the subject or the camera's height directly in the illustration, not just with sliders.
  • Works offlineEverything is bundled in the app — no signal needed once it's installed.

Install on your phone

  1. Tap Download for Android above. It downloads a single .apk file (about 2.5 MB) straight from GitHub.

  2. Open the downloaded file from your notification shade or Downloads app.Android will offer to open it with Package Installer — that's the right one.

  3. Android will warn that installing from this source isn't allowed yet and offer a Settings shortcut. Turn on "Allow from this source" for your browser — it's a one-time step.

  4. Go back and tap Install, then open PhotoSim.

This warning appears because PhotoSim isn't distributed through the Play Store — it's a small independent project. The APK is built straight from the source in this repository, nothing hidden.
